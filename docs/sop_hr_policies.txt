COMPANY LEAVE POLICY - STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Document ID: HR-LVE-001 | Version: 2.0 | Effective Date: January 2025
Applies to: All full-time and part-time employees

1. ANNUAL / PAID LEAVE
1.1 Every employee is entitled to 18 paid leave (vacation) days per calendar year.
1.2 Leave days are accrued monthly at a rate of 1.5 days per month of service.
1.3 Up to 6 unused leave days may be carried forward into the next calendar year. Carried-forward days expire on March 31 of the following year.
1.4 Leave applications must be submitted through the HR portal at least 7 calendar days in advance.
1.5 Leave of 5 or more consecutive working days requires approval from both the reporting manager and the HR department.
1.6 Unused leave days at the end of the year, beyond the carry-forward limit, will be encashed at the employee's basic daily rate.
1.7 Employees on probation are eligible for 1 paid leave day per month of probation.

2. SICK LEAVE
2.1 Employees are entitled to 12 paid sick days per calendar year.
2.2 Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner.
2.3 Sick leave must be reported to the reporting manager before 10:00 AM on the day of absence.
2.4 Unused sick days do not carry forward and are not encashed.

3. MATERNITY LEAVE
3.1 Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.
3.2 Maternity leave may be taken up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.
3.3 An additional 1 month of paid leave is granted in case of medical complications related to pregnancy.

4. PATERNITY LEAVE
4.1 Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave.
4.2 Paternity leave must be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

5. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
5.1 The company observes 12 public holidays per calendar year.
5.2 Employees required to work on a public holiday receive compensatory off or double pay.

6. UNPAID LEAVE
6.1 Unpaid leave may be granted for up to 30 days in exceptional circumstances with HR approval.
6.2 Unpaid leave does not count toward tenure accrual.

7. LEAVE DURING NOTICE PERIOD
7.1 Employees serving a notice period may avail of accrued paid leave at the discretion of the reporting manager.
7.2 Unavailed leave during the notice period will be encashed in the final settlement.

8. WORK FROM HOME (WFH)
8.1 Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
8.2 Fully remote work for more than 15 days requires HR approval.

9. EMERGENCY CONTACT
9.1 For leave-related queries, contact the HR helpdesk at hr@company.com or extension 4501.
