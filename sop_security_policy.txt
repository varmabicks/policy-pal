COMPANY HR POLICY - STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Document ID: HR-POL-001 | Version: 3.1 | Effective Date: January 2025
Applies to: All employees

1. WORKING HOURS & ATTENDANCE
1.1 Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday.
1.2 A 30-minute lunch break is provided between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM.
1.3 Employees must log attendance using the biometric system or the company web portal.
1.4 A grace period of 15 minutes is allowed for arrival. Arrival after the grace period is marked as "Late."
1.5 Three or more late arrivals in a calendar month trigger a formal review meeting with the reporting manager.
1.6 Employees must complete a minimum of 8 working hours per day. Overtime for eligible grades is compensated at 1.25x the hourly rate.

2. DRESS CODE
2.1 Business casual attire is mandatory Monday through Thursday.
2.2 Casual dress is permitted on Fridays, subject to client meetings (business formal required on those days).
2.3 Revealing clothing, flip-flops, and clothing with offensive graphics are not permitted on any day.

3. PROBATION
3.1 New employees serve a 3-month probation period, extendable by up to 3 months based on performance.
3.2 During probation, notice period is 2 weeks. After confirmation, notice period is 60 days.
3.3 Probation confirmation is communicated in writing by the HR department.

4. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
4.1 Performance reviews are conducted twice a year: mid-year in July and annual in December.
4.2 Ratings are on a 5-point scale: Outstanding, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement, Unsatisfactory.
4.3 Two consecutive "Needs Improvement" or one "Unsatisfactory" rating initiates a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) of 60 days.
4.4 Increment letters are released in the first week of January.

5. SALARY & BENEFITS
5.1 Salary is credited on the last working day of each month.
5.2 Benefits include health insurance (employee + spouse + 2 children), annual wellness reimbursement of 300, and a learning & development budget of500 per year.
5.3 Employees are eligible for an annual bonus based on company performance and individual rating.

6. CODE OF CONDUCT
6.1 Discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind is strictly prohibited and leads to disciplinary action up to termination.
6.2 Complaints may be reported to the Ethics Committee at ethics@company.com; all reports are treated confidentially.
6.3 Alcohol and smoking are prohibited on company premises.
6.4 Personal social media posts disclosing confidential company information violate this policy.

7. EXIT PROCESS
7.1 Resignation must be submitted in writing to the reporting manager and HR.
7.2 The notice period is 60 days for confirmed employees.
7.3 Full and final settlement is completed within 45 days of the last working day.
7.4 Employees must return all company assets (laptop, ID card, access cards) on or before the last working day.

8. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
8.1 Employees may raise grievances to their reporting manager or HR.
8.2 Grievances are acknowledged within 3 working days and resolved within 15 working days.

9. CONTACT
9.1 HR helpdesk: hr@company.com, extension 4501.
